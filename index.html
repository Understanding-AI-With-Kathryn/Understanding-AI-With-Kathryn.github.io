Ordering / Service FAQ

What's the AI Jumpstart Call?

A single 60-minute working session — no written plan included. Good for picking one thing to fix and leaving with a clear next step, or for deciding whether Blueprint or Build fits before you commit to either.

What do I get with Blueprint?

A written plan covering your client journey, onboarding boundaries, and a 30-day setup plan — yours to implement on your own timeline.

What do I get with Build?

Everything in Blueprint, plus Kathryn's direct setup work: three scheduled routines built and running, a training walkthrough, a welcome and setup record, and a two-week follow-up to catch anything that needs adjusting.

How many Build clients do you take at once?

One at a time, so each setup gets full attention before the next one starts.

How do I start?

Choose a tier above and book it through Gumroad, or email understandingaiwithkathryn@gmail.com first if you'd rather talk it through. You can also fill out the intake questionnaire before or after booking — it emails your answers to Kathryn, but booking and payment only happen through Gumroad.

What are the payment, cancellation, and privacy terms?

See the full terms below before you book; email Kathryn first if anything is unclear.

Is my data private?

Setup happens in accounts you own and control. Kathryn doesn't ask for or store your passwords or account credentials, and this work does not require sensitive medical, financial, or credential information. Full written privacy terms are below.

Payment, cancellation & privacy

Payment

  • AI Jumpstart Call: full payment due at booking, through Gumroad.
  • Blueprint: pay in two installments through Gumroad — half at checkout, half charged automatically to your card afterward.
  • Build: pay in two installments through Gumroad — half at checkout, half charged automatically to your card afterward.

Cancellation & rescheduling

You may reschedule once at no charge with at least 48 hours' notice. Cancelling with less than 48 hours' notice forfeits the first installment already paid. Cancelling more than 7 days before your scheduled session gets a full refund. A no-show is treated as a late cancellation.

Privacy

Kathryn does not collect or store account passwords or login credentials. All setup happens in accounts you own. This work does not require sensitive medical, financial, or credential information, and session notes use non-sensitive or fictional examples where possible. Your contact information and session notes are kept only as long as needed to deliver the work and are never sold or shared with third parties.
